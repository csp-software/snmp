About Digital Inspiration

Digital Inspiration is dedicated to publishing technical reference books, educational workbooks, academic guides, and facilitating online workshop registrations for students and professionals.

Legal & Compliance Policies

1. Terms & Conditions

By placing an order or registering on Digital Inspiration, you confirm that all submitted details (including shipping address or participant info) are accurate. Orders are non-transferable, and no refund will be provided once payment is confirmed and the form is submitted.

2. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Once a payment is confirmed and the order/registration form is submitted, cancellations are not permitted. However, if an amount is debited and the order or form submission fails due to a technical issue, a full refund will be processed to the original payment method within 7 working days.

3. Privacy Policy

We collect only your name, selected book/activity, payment reference ID, and form responses for order fulfillment and registration management. All payment credentials are processed securely by Razorpay and are never stored on our servers.

4. Shipping & Delivery Policy

Physical books ordered through this portal are dispatched within 3–5 working days via registered courier/India Post and typically delivered within 5–7 working days across India. Digital forms and online registrations unlock immediately upon payment confirmation.
